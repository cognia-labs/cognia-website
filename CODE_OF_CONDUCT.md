# Community code of conduct

Everyone participating in Cognia Labs projects is expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive, including when disagreeing.
- Welcome people across experience levels, backgrounds and identities.
- Respect privacy, consent, intellectual property and contributor attribution.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized behavior, doxxing and spam.
- Keep discussion relevant to the project and accept reasonable moderation.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, restrict participation, or block repeat or severe violations. Apply moderation proportionately and protect reporter privacy.

For conduct concerns, contact [@MaxQian888](https://github.com/MaxQian888) to arrange a private reporting channel. Do not publish sensitive reports in public issues. Maintainers should not adjudicate reports about themselves; if no independent maintainer is available, GitHub's platform reporting and blocking tools remain available.
